### Switching to a custom electricity tariff

Switching from Octopus or another provider to Other / Custom TOU now saves the new provider selection along with your rates, so PowerSync stops using the previous pricing provider after reload.

If you already tried switching and PowerSync kept using the old provider, update, reopen Configure > Electricity pricing & provider, select Other / Custom TOU, and finish saving your tariff again. Reinstalling the integration is not required.
