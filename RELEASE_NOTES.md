## Choose which Tesla charge schedules to plan

A new “Plan Tesla charge schedules” switch under EVs & Chargers controls each car’s imported Tesla windows independently of Smart EV Charging. It defaults on, keeping future home-charge demand in the plan while away or unplugged. Turn it off during a longer absence when no home charge is expected.

The choice survives restarts and affects that car only. Turning it off excludes its cached Tesla schedule forecast and pauses further schedule reads; turning it on resumes planning. Tesla’s actual schedules, PowerSync’s own Smart Schedule settings and native Charge on Solar ownership remain unchanged.
