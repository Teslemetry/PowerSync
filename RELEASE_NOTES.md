## What's Changed

**PowerSync loads correctly after updating**

Fixed a startup error introduced in v2.12.1451 that could prevent PowerSync from loading, even when native Charge on Solar was unused. The failed setup could also leave the EV Charging card alternating between unavailable and loading messages.

Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant to load the corrected files.
