## What's Changed

**Compare today's energy use with buy and sell prices**
The Home Assistant “Energy - 24 Hours” card now includes optional Buy Price and Sell Price lines. Both are hidden by default; select their legend buttons to show them alongside power and battery level, with a separate price scale.

The chart follows Home Assistant's timezone. Price lines show available recorded sensor history up to now, including negative prices. Missing history is not reconstructed from the current tariff, and future hours remain blank for prices.

Update available via HACS
