## Battery-only sites

Adds **No solar installed** under **Configure > Advanced settings > Weather & solar forecast**. Tick it and save if your property has no solar panels: PowerSync will plan with zero solar and stop showing the missing solar forecast warning.

Saved forecast settings are retained. Untick the option when solar is installed to resume normal forecasting. The matching mobile switch requires PowerSync Mobile build 444 or later.
