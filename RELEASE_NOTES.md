## What's Changed

**Clearer Tesla force-discharge cleanup status**
After a failed Force Discharge enters restoration, the Battery Mode sensor now explains that the command was not confirmed and saved settings are being restored, instead of claiming the battery is discharging. Cleanup remains active until restoration is verified; this display correction does not remove Tesla/Teslemetry upload limits.

Update available via HACS
