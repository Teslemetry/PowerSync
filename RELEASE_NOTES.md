## What's Changed

**HA optimiser subscriptions and activation**
The local HA optimiser now supports a separate A$4/month subscription, or an active recurring GitHub sponsorship of US$3/month or more. Existing qualifying sponsors are included; monitoring, dashboards and manual controls remain free, and PowerSync Cloud remains a separate subscription.

**Activate before 23 October**
Subscription access will be required from **23 October 2026 at 00:00 UTC (11:00 am AEDT)**. Open the integration options → **Optimiser access** → **Start pairing**, sign in at [the HA account page](https://powersync.cc/app/ha), approve your code, then return to HA and choose **Finish pairing**. See the [activation guide](https://powersync.cc/ha-optimiser) for billing, cancellation and moving an installation.

**Choose one payment option**
Direct signup requires a card and includes one seven-day trial, then automatically bills A$4/month until cancelled. GitHub sponsorship bills immediately without a trial. Paid HA subscribers and qualifying active sponsors can link Discord for priority HA optimiser support; no response-time guarantee is included.

**Clear access status and safe recovery**
HA shows pairing, permission expiry and recovery status. When access ends, new optimiser actions pause and PowerSync attempts to restore controls it owns while preserving manual controls and newer charging requests. After an access gap, refresh access and explicitly choose **Resume optimisation** once recovery is complete.
